Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the AI Advisor?
Tap the "Ask AI" button in the top-right corner of any screen. You'll need a free OpenRouter account and API key to use this feature. Visit openrouter.ai to sign up.
Why am I seeing ads?
The free version of Mortgage Analyzer AI is supported by ads. Upgrade to Mortgage Analyzer AI Pro (one-time purchase) to remove all ads permanently.
How do I restore my Pro purchase?
Tap the Upgrade button anywhere in the app, then tap "Restore Purchase" at the bottom of the screen.
How do I save a mortgage scenario?
Tap the save icon in the toolbar on the Calculator screen. Your scenario will be saved as a .mortgage file that you can reload anytime.
What is the Canadian mortgage convention?
Canadian mortgages compound interest semi-annually rather than monthly. Enable this option in the Mortgage Information section if you're calculating a Canadian mortgage.
How accurate are the AI Advisor's responses?
The AI Advisor provides general information based on your current loan scenario. Responses are for informational purposes only and should not be considered financial advice. Always verify important decisions with a qualified mortgage professional.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
Please email us at ma-support@colemanfolks.com with a description of the issue and your device model and iOS version.
