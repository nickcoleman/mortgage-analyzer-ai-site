Designed for decisions, not quick guesses

The app already goes far beyond a one-number mortgage estimate. The website should reflect that depth, so this version leans into the real workflow buyers use when evaluating affordability, payoff speed, and loan risk.

1. Start With The Real Payment

Lead with the hero card, not a plain form.

The app foregrounds a premium payment summary card with principal, interest, MI, taxes, insurance, HOA, LTV, and APR. That gives the product an immediate “finance tool” feel instead of a generic web calculator feel.

2. Explore Tradeoffs

Stress-test the loan from multiple angles.

Users can move through mortgage info, extra payments, loan type, and monthly cost sections without losing context. The website now mirrors that layered exploration rather than flattening everything into a single sentence.

3. Understand The Outcome

Charts and amortization make the math tangible.

The app is strongest when it translates calculations into visible payoff curves and payment-by-payment detail. That visual payoff is now part of the homepage pitch instead of an afterthought.

What the app actually covers

These sections are grounded in the current SwiftUI product, not a future wish list. They map directly to the calculator tabs, sheets, and analysis tools already in the app.

Monthly Cost Breakdown

Move beyond principal and interest.

Property tax, homeowners insurance, HOA, PMI or MIP, and even misc monthly costs can be included so the “real” payment is visible up front. DTI inputs help buyers connect the payment to actual affordability.

Chart View

See how balance and payoff timing change.

The chart turns abstract loan math into something you can scan in seconds. It is especially useful when testing extra payments or comparing how aggressive payoff strategies bend the curve.

Amortization Schedule

Inspect the loan payment by payment.

Browse grouped yearly sections, search by year or payment number, spot PMI removal, and even add one-time extra payments on individual rows. This is not just a teaser table; it is a working analysis view.

Tablet-Ready Workflow

Built for iPhone, iPad, and a more expansive planning session.

The app adapts to larger screens with a multi-panel layout so buyers can work through mortgage info, extra payments, monthly costs, and loan type in a more deliberate way.

A complete mortgage workflow in one app

The strongest conversion story is not “we have a calculator.” It is “we help you work through the mortgage from setup, to tradeoff analysis, to payoff visibility, to plain-language explanation.”

Built for people asking “what if?”

The app is at its best when users need to answer practical questions before a purchase or refinance decision.

  • What happens if I put another 5% down?
  • How much interest disappears if I add $200 per month?
  • How exposed am I when an ARM adjusts?
  • When does PMI fall off?
  • What does this payment look like once taxes and insurance are included?

Already set up for more premium tools

The codebase includes a clear roadmap toward comparison tools, PDF export, refinance analysis, affordability workflows, and more advanced scenarios. That gives the product room to grow without feeling like a dead-end calculator.

AI is a helper, not the whole pitch

The current site puts too much weight on AI setup. This version treats AI as a useful layer on top of a strong mortgage product, which makes the page feel more trustworthy and more durable.

Ask for explanations in plain English

The AI Advisor helps users interpret the numbers they already entered. It is good for questions about payment drivers, ARM risk, interest savings, and payoff timing.

Nothing to set up, nothing to send

The AI Advisor runs on your device using Apple Intelligence. There is no account to create, no API key to enter and no subscription — and nothing you type is sent to us or to any third-party AI service.

  • Private on-device assistance, which works offline.
  • No account, no API key, no cloud AI service.
  • The conversation stays in memory and clears when you close the app.
  • Support page available for questions and troubleshooting.
