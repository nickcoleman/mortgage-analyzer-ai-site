How to connect the AI Advisor

The AI feature is optional. You bring your own OpenRouter API key, save it in the app, and your requests go directly from your device to OpenRouter.

1

Create an OpenRouter account

Visit openrouter.ai and sign in. If you are new to the service, complete account setup first.

2

Generate an API key

Open your OpenRouter dashboard, create a new API key, and copy it immediately. Store it somewhere safe until you paste it into the app.

3

Paste it into Mortgage Analyzer AI

In the app, open the AI settings screen, paste the key, and save. The key stays on your device in the Keychain and is not sent to the developer.
