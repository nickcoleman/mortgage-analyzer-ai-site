Privacy Policy

Mortgage Analyzer AI
Last updated: April 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information Mortgage Analyzer AI collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Do Not Collect

Mortgage Analyzer AI does not require an account, does not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information, and does not store your mortgage data on any server. All loan calculations are performed entirely on your device.

Advertising (AdMob)

The free version of this app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (including Apple's Advertising Identifier, or IDFA) to show you relevant ads. When you first open the app, you will be asked for permission to track via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You may decline this permission and ads will still be shown, but they will not be personalized.

For more information on how Google uses data in advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy.

AI Advisor (Optional Feature)

If you choose to enable the AI Advisor, you will need to provide an API key from OpenRouter. This key is stored securely in your device's Keychain and is never transmitted to us. When you send a message in the AI Advisor, that message and your current loan details are sent directly from your device to OpenRouter's servers for processing. We do not see, store, or have access to your AI conversations. Please review OpenRouter's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle your data.

Data Storage

Any mortgage scenarios you save are stored locally on your device as files. If you use iCloud Drive, these files may sync across your devices via Apple's iCloud service, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your saved files.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the support page.
